Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 6, 2026

Overview

Bond Assistant ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal AI assistant created by Vardges Arutyunyan for private use.

Data Collection

This application collects health and fitness data from WHOOP solely for personal use by the account owner. Data includes:

  • Recovery scores and metrics
  • Sleep data (duration, quality, stages)
  • Strain and workout information
  • Heart rate and HRV data
  • Profile and body measurements

Data Usage

All collected data is used exclusively for:

  • Personal health insights and summaries
  • Private notifications and recommendations
  • Trend analysis for the account owner

Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or distribute any personal data to third parties. All data remains private and is accessible only to the account owner.

Data Storage

Data is stored securely on private servers and is not shared publicly.
